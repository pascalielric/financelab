Project Lancy: take private of Temenos AG

Illustrative sponsor buyout of the Swiss core banking software leader. Temenos has attracted repeated private equity interest; this model tests what a financial buyer could pay and still earn a target return. The answer is disciplined: the base case does not support a customary premium. Figures in USD millions (Temenos reporting currency); the offer price is in CHF.

06Investment thesis and key risks

Why the asset suits a buyout

  • Mission critical core banking software with annual recurring revenue (ARR) of USD 904m at Q3 2026, growing 12% in constant currency
  • Recurring product revenue is about 91% of total product revenue, which supports leverage of 6.0x EBITDA
  • Non IFRS EBIT margin of 34.7% in FY25 with management targeting EBIT of about USD 480m by FY28; the base case runs modestly ahead of that path, the upside case well ahead
  • Cash conversion: FY25 free cash flow of USD 256m, guided to grow about 16% in FY26
  • Clear exits: sale to a strategic acquirer, a larger software sponsor, or a relisting

What could go wrong

  • Price: the board rejected earlier approaches on valuation; at a customary 25% premium the base case returns about 14%, and above a premium of about 22% it falls below 15%
  • Multiple compression: one turn of exit multiple moves IRR by about 1.5 points
  • Subscription transition and deal timing make quarterly revenue lumpy (Q2 2026 saw deals slip into Q3)
  • Cloud native challengers and in house builds at tier one banks
  • Floating rate exposure on the term loan; USD revenue against a CHF cost base and CHF equity cheque
