Valuation, Transactions and Portfolio Risk

Five working projects covering the core toolkit of investment banking, private equity and asset management: a discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation, a leveraged buyout (LBO), Monte Carlo simulation, portfolio optimisation with value at risk, and earnings analysis. Every model is interactive, every assumption is visible, and both transaction cases come with a presentation ready pitch deck and a formula driven Excel model.

04Approach

Why these cases

Food science and economics let me read a business from both sides: the science that creates the product and the economics that create the value. That is why the valuation covers Givaudan, whose flavours business sits exactly at that intersection, and why the buyout looks at Temenos, a Swiss software company with the recurring revenue a sponsor needs.

Assumptions are sourced, judgement calls are explained, and each model can be stress tested live.

How it is built

Static HTML and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages, charts with Plotly. The valuation engines are plain JavaScript functions shared by the website, the Excel models and the decks, so every number ties out across formats.

Prices and earnings data are refreshed every weekday by an automated pipeline (Python, Yahoo Finance, GitHub Actions). The note at the top right shows the date of the last refresh.
