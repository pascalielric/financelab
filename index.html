03About

Background

I am a Master's student in Management, Technology and Economics at ETH Zurich with a BSc in Food Science, also from ETH. The combination lets me read a business from both sides: the science that creates the product and the economics that create the value. That is why the DCF covers Givaudan, whose flavours business sits exactly at that intersection.

I built this site to show how I think about valuation and risk, not only that I can fill in a template: assumptions are sourced, judgement calls are explained, and each model can be stress tested live.

How it is built

Static HTML and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages, charts with Plotly. The valuation engines are plain JavaScript functions shared by the website, the Excel models and the decks, so every number ties out across formats.

Prices and earnings data are refreshed every weekday by an automated pipeline (Python, yfinance, GitHub Actions). The badge in the header shows the date of the last refresh.
