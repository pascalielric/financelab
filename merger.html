10What would stand in the way

Control and Swiss company law

  • On has two share classes. Ten Class B voting rights shares carry the economic rights of one Class A share but ten times the votes. At the end of 2025 there were 296.9m Class A shares and 341.2m Class B shares, almost all held by the founders and early team, who therefore control a majority of the votes. No deal happens without them.
  • On Holding AG is a Swiss company listed only in New York. Swiss takeover rules apply to companies with a main listing in Switzerland, so the deal would run as a US tender offer followed by a merger under the Swiss Merger Act.
  • A merger needs two thirds of the votes represented and a majority of the nominal capital represented. Because Class A shares carry ten times the nominal value of Class B shares, both the founders and the public shareholders effectively have to agree. A merger that pays On holders only in cash needs 90% of all votes, which is one reason a part in Nike shares helps.
  • Nike is itself controlled through its Class A shares, held mainly by the Knight family's Swoosh LLC, so a stock deal would not change control at Nike.

Antitrust, brand and execution

  • Antitrust: Nike is one of the largest running brands; the FTC (Federal Trade Commission) and the European Commission would review the deal closely. The market is crowded (Hoka, Asics, Brooks, New Balance, Adidas, Saucony), which helps the case for clearance, but remedies on wholesale terms are possible.
  • Brand: On's value is its premium, independent image. The Converse model (owned since 2003, run as a separate brand) is the template; forcing On into Nike's distribution would destroy what Nike pays for.
  • People: the founders run On as co chief executives since May 2026. Retention and earn out terms would be central to the negotiation.
  • Currency: On's costs and reporting are partly in Swiss francs while its sales are mostly in US dollars and euros, so Nike would take on a new currency exposure.
  • Timing: Nike is guiding to a high single digit revenue decline in FY27 while On grows about 20%. Investors may read the deal as buying growth instead of fixing the core, or as the fastest way to win back running.
  • Strategy: at its Investor Day on 17 Nov 2026 Nike reaffirmed its FY27 guidance and set 2031 targets of about USD 5bn of extra revenue, an operating margin of 12% to 14% and adjusted earnings per share of USD 3.00 to 3.50. A large acquisition would sit awkwardly next to that plan, which raises the bar for the board.

11Methodology

How the model works

Pro forma net income is Nike's standalone net income plus On's net income converted into US dollars, plus after tax synergies, minus after tax interest on new debt, minus interest Nike no longer earns on the cash it uses, minus amortisation of the intangible assets created by the deal and of financing fees. Dividing by Nike's shares plus the new shares issued to On holders gives pro forma earnings per share.

Nike's fiscal year ends in May; On reports by calendar year. On's figures are calendarised: Nike's FY28 (June 2027 to May 2028) takes 7/12 of On's 2027 and 5/12 of its 2028.

Goodwill is the purchase price above On's book equity after writing up the brand (not amortised) and customer relationships and technology (amortised over ten years), with a deferred tax liability on the write ups.

Inputs and judgement calls

Earnings per share forecasts are analyst consensus where available (Nike FY28, On 2026 and 2027) and the author's extrapolation beyond that, marked in the tables. Nike's consensus is on a reported basis; On's is adjusted, which flatters On slightly.

Cost synergies of USD 250m are built bottom up from sourcing, logistics, wholesale terms and duplicated listed company costs, about 4.5% of On's sales. Revenue synergies are left out of the base case, as most banks do, but can be added with the slider.

On has no financial debt; its lease liabilities are left out of net cash for both companies. New debt costs the five year Treasury yield plus 75 basis points for an A rated borrower.
